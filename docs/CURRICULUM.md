# LENTERA 2.0 — CURRICULUM SYSTEM

## Curriculum Hierarchy
```
Board (CBSE / Odisha)
  └── Class (Class 10)
        └── Subject (Science / Mathematics)
              └── Chapter (e.g. Light - Reflection & Refraction)
                    └── Topic (e.g. Refraction of Light)
                          └── Concept (e.g. Lens Formula & Magnification)
```

## Supported Curriculum Data
1. **CBSE Class 10 Science**:
   * Light — Reflection and Refraction (Reflection laws, Spherical mirrors, Mirror formula, Refraction, Snell's law, Lenses, Lens formula, Magnification, Power of lens).
   * Human Eye and Colourful World (Prism dispersion, Atmospheric refraction, Scattering of light).
   * Chemical Reactions and Equations.
   * Electricity & Magnetic Effects.
2. **CBSE Class 10 Mathematics**:
   * Real Numbers & Polynomials.
   * Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables.
   * Quadratic Equations & Arithmetic Progressions.
   * Triangles & Coordinate Geometry.
3. **Odisha State Board Class 10**:
   * Equivalent topics structured for regional state curriculum.

Each concept includes prerequisites, difficulty level, learning objectives, source citations, question templates, and mastery thresholds.
