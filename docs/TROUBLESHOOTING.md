# LENTERA 2.0 — TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

## Common Issues & Solutions

### 1. Camera Preview blank
* **Cause**: Missing camera permission.
* **Solution**: Ensure Camera permission is granted in Android system settings or via prompt.

### 2. Speech recognition not working in Airplane mode
* **Cause**: Device offline speech recognition model not downloaded on device.
* **Solution**: The app displays fallback message (*"Offline voice recognition isn't available for this language on this device. You can type instead."*). Typing works 100% offline.

### 3. Classroom connection fails
* **Cause**: Teacher and student devices are on different networks.
* **Solution**: Ensure student device connects to Teacher device's local Wi-Fi or Portable Hotspot. No internet required.
